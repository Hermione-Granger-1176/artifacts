Every name and number here is invented
The vendors, buyers, addresses, phone numbers, and tax identifiers are fabricated for sample data and do not refer to real businesses. Documents are marked as samples and are not valid invoices, receipts, or tax records. Sales tax is applied at a flat 8.25% for illustration and is not a real jurisdiction's rate.
How the randomisation works
Every document is driven by a single integer seed. Changing the vendor, type, or layout re-renders against the same seed so you can compare treatments side by side; Generate new document is what rolls a fresh seed. The seed appears on the preview and in every exported filename, so any sample can be reproduced exactly. Scan degradation is driven from the same seed, so a given seed and preset always produce the same page, and the boxes follow the ink when the page is tilted.
