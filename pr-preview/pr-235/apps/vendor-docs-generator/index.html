Every name and number here is invented
The vendors, buyers, addresses, phone numbers, and tax identifiers are fabricated for sample data and do not refer to real businesses. Documents are marked as samples and are not valid invoices, receipts, or tax records. Sales tax is applied at a flat 8.25% for illustration and is not a real jurisdiction's rate.

How the randomisation works
Within a run, every random choice is driven by a single integer seed, while document dates are relative to the day of generation. Changing the vendor, type, or layout re-renders against the same seed so you can compare treatments side by side; New document is what rolls a fresh seed. The seed appears in the toolbar and in every exported filename so each sample remains identifiable. Exact replay in code also needs the original reference date, which can be recovered from the sidecar's document date and the seed-derived date offsets. Scan degradation is driven from the same seed, so a given seed and preset reproduce the same wear, and the boxes follow the ink when the page is tilted.
